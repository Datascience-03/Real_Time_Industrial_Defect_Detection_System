GET /health

POST /plc/send
  - REST PLC communication handles up to 1,152 RPS at concurrency=10.

POST /predict (YOLO ONNX Inference)
  - Single-thread throughput: ~14.86 RPS with avg latency of 67.11ms per image.
  - ONNX inference is CPU-bound (CPUExecutionProvider); throughput plateaus at ~15 RPS
    regardless of concurrency since the GIL serialises prediction calls.
  - For industrial real-time targets of 30+ FPS, TensorRT GPU acceleration is recommended
    (as verified in Week 3: 146.15 FPS on NVIDIA RTX 3050).
  - P99 latency at concurrency=1: 76.84ms — well within acceptable range for single-stream
    inspection lines running at standard 15 FPS camera framerates.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
  1. Deploy with TensorRT engine for production-grade throughput (10x speedup verified).
  2. Scale /health and /plc/send horizontally; they are stateless and highly concurrent.
  3. Set uvicorn workers > 1 for multi-stream /predict to utilize multi-core CPU headroom.
